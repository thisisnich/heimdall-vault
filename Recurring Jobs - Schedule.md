# Recurring Jobs - Maintenance Schedule

> 🔄 **System Maintenance** — Daily, nightly, and weekly automation
> 🎯 **Goal:** Keep vault healthy without making maintenance a job

---

## 📅 Daily Jobs

### Morning Setup (5 minutes)
**Time:** First thing each morning  
**Purpose:** Set up for productive day

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Open [[02-DAILY/{{date}}|Today's Daily Note]]
- [ ] Quick scan [[Resources-RAW/inbox/|Inbox]] for urgent items
- [ ] Review [[📅 Schedule & Assessments Dashboard|Schedule]] for deadlines
- [ ] Set [[Projects/Projects - Index|Project Priorities]] for today
- [ ] Check [[Areas/Areas - Index|Area Focus]] for current priorities

**Outputs:**
- ✅ Clear daily focus
- ✅ Urgent items identified
- ✅ Schedule awareness
- ✅ Priority alignment

---

### Throughout Day (As needed)
**Purpose:** Maintain momentum and capture

**Actions:**
- **Capture:** All inputs to [[Resources-RAW/inbox/|Inbox]]
- **Quick Process:** Move obvious items immediately
- **Link Building:** Connect new ideas to existing knowledge
- **Task Updates:** Mark completed items in project notes

**Rule:** Never break flow for organization - capture and continue

---

### Evening Review (10 minutes)
**Time:** End of work day  
**Purpose:** Daily cleanup and preparation

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Process [[Resources-RAW/inbox/|Inbox]] (target: < 5 items remaining)
- [ ] Update [[Projects/Projects - Index|Project Progress]]
- [ ] Record key learnings in [[02-DAILY/{{date}}|Daily Note]]
- [ ] Create [[02-DAILY/{{date+1}}|Tomorrow's Daily Note]]
- [ ] Quick [[Areas/Areas - Index|Area Check-in]]

**Outputs:**
- ✅ Clean inbox
- ✅ Updated project status
- ✅ Captured learnings
- ✅ Tomorrow prepared
- ✅ Area health check

## 📊 Weekly Jobs

### Friday Review (60 minutes)
**Time:** Friday evening or Saturday morning  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive weekly maintenance

**Phase 1: Capture Processing (20 min)**
- [ ] Deep dive [[Resources-RAW/inbox/|Inbox]] (target: empty)
- [ ] Categorize remaining items to PARA locations
- [ ] Delete obvious junk and duplicates
- [ ] Flag items needing research or synthesis

**Phase 2: Project Review (15 min)**
- [ ] Update all [[Projects/Projects - Index|Active Projects]] status
- [ ] Review project deadlines and milestones
- [ ] Move completed projects to [[04-ARCHIVE/|Archive]]
- [ ] Identify project blockers and next steps

**Phase 3: Area Assessment (10 min)**
- [ ] Review [[Areas/Areas - Index|All Areas]] health
- [ ] Adjust time allocation if needed
- [ ] Update area goals and metrics
- [ ] Identify new areas to develop

**Phase 4: Knowledge Synthesis (10 min)**
- [ ] Create 2-3 structured notes from raw material
- [ ] Strengthen knowledge connections
- [ ] Update [[wiki.md|Knowledge Base]] with new insights
- [ ] Identify content gaps for future research

**Phase 5: System Maintenance (5 min)**
- [ ] [[04-ARCHIVE/|Archive]] outdated materials
- [ ] Clean up broken links and orphan notes
- [ ] Update [[PARA - Index|PARA metrics]] and dashboards
- [ ] Set next week's priorities

**Outputs:**
- ✅ Empty inbox
- ✅ Updated project status
- ✅ Healthy areas
- ✅ New knowledge created
- ✅ Clean system
- ✅ Clear priorities

## 🔄 Job Adaptation Rules

### When to Adjust Schedule
**Increase Frequency If:**
- Inbox consistently > 10 items
- Project status becomes outdated
- Knowledge creation falls behind
- System health issues arise

**Decrease Frequency If:**
- Jobs consistently finish early
- System maintenance is minimal
- Workflow is highly efficient
- Time constraints require optimization

**Change Job Content If:**
- New tools or workflows introduced
- Project types change significantly
- Learning priorities shift
- System structure evolves

### Continuous Improvement
**Monthly Review Questions:**
- Are jobs completing on time?
- Is system health improving?
- Are workflows becoming more efficient?
- What needs adjustment next month?

**Optimization Areas:**
- **Time Management:** Reduce job duration while maintaining quality
- **Automation:** Increase automated components
- **Effectiveness:** Improve job outcomes and impact
- **Enjoyment:** Make maintenance less burdensome
