## 🔄 Processing Workflow

### Daily Ingest (5-10 minutes)
1. **Quick Review:** Scan inbox for obvious items
2. **Easy Wins:** Move clearly categorized items
3. **Flag Important:** Mark items needing attention
4. **Clear Clutter:** Delete obvious junk

### Weekly Processing (30-60 minutes)
1. **Deep Review:** Go through remaining inbox items
2. **Categorize:** Move to appropriate locations
3. **Connect:** Link related concepts
4. **Synthesize:** Create new structured notes

### Monthly Curation (2-3 hours)
1. **Resource Audit:** Review all organized materials
2. **Connection Building:** Strengthen knowledge links
3. **Gap Identification:** Find missing information
4. **Archive Management:** Move outdated items

---

## 🎯 Processing Guidelines

### When to Promote from Inbox
**Immediate Promotion:**
- Reference materials with clear purpose
- Action items with specific next steps
- Course materials with clear categorization

**Weekly Processing:**
- Complex ideas needing synthesis
- Research materials requiring connection
- Multi-source information needing integration

**Monthly Review:**
- Archive-worthy materials
- Outdated references
- Duplicate or redundant information

### Quality Standards
**Good Resource:**
- ✅ Clear purpose and context
- ✅ Properly tagged and categorized
- ✅ Connected to related concepts
- ✅ Accessible and searchable

**Needs Work:**
- ⚠️ Unclear purpose or context
- ⚠️ Poorly tagged or uncategorized
- ⚠️ Isolated from related concepts
- ⚠️ Hard to find or access
