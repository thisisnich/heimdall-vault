1)  Define  wireless  communication.  What  are  the  advantages  and  disadvantages  of

wireless communication?

Wireless  communication  involves  the  transmission  of  information  over  a  distance
without the need of wires, cables or any other forms of electrical conductors in between
the transmitter and receiver.

Advantages

•  A wireless communication network is a solution in areas where cables are

impossible to install.

•  Wireless networks are cheaper to install and maintain.
•  Flexible

Disadvantages

•  Has security vulnerabilities.
•  High cost for setting the infrastructure.
•  Unlike wired communication, wireless communication is influenced by physical
obstructions, climatic conditions and interference from other wireless devices.

2)  Name and explain the three types of transmission modes in data communication and

their applications.

YouTube link
Simplex, Half duplex and Full duplex, the different types of transmission modes - YouTube

Simplex
Information travels in one direction only
TV and Radio broadcast

Half duplex
Information travels in both directions but one direction at a time => at any one time
only one party can speak and the other party must list
Walkie Talkie

Full duplex
Information travels in both directions simultaneously => both parties can speak at the
same time
Mobile Phone

3)  Draw the basic building block of an RF system and explain briefly the function of each

block.

Low frequency information signal – The signal that you intend to send over

High  frequency  carrier  –  The  purpose  of  the  carrier  is  usually  either  to  transmit  the
information through air or to allow several carriers at different frequencies to share a common
physical transmission medium.

Modulator – An RF modulator (or radio frequency modulator) is an electronic device whose
input is a baseband signal which is used to modulate a radio frequency source.

Amplifier - An amplifier functions by using electric power from a power supply to increase
the amplitude of the voltage or current signal. An amplifier is effectively the opposite of an
attenuator: while an amplifier provides gain, an attenuator provides loss.

Communication channel – A medium through which a message is transmitted to its intended
audience.

Demodulator – A device used to recover the original modulating signal from a modulated
wave. A demodulator is also known as a detector.

Output  transducer  -  A  device  which  can  convert  information  from  one  physical  form  to
another.  The  output  transducer  transfers  signals  out  of  the  electrical  domain  and  into  the
domain that can be perceived by one of the five human senses.
