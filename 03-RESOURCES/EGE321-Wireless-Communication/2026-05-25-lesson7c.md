## Slide 9 — QPSK variants

![[99-ATTACHMENTS/EGE321/lesson7c/lesson7c-slide-09.png|Slide 9 — Conventional vs π/4 vs Offset QPSK]]

| Scheme | Key property |
|--------|--------------|
| **Conventional QPSK** | Transitions through zero (180° phase jumps) |
| **π/4 QPSK** | Constellation toggles each symbol — **no transitions through zero** → lowest envelope variation |
| **Offset QPSK (OQPSK)** | I and Q transitions **staggered**; phase steps limited to **90°** |

## Slide 15 — BPSK modulator operation

![[99-ATTACHMENTS/EGE321/lesson7c/lesson7c-slide-15.png|Slide 15 — BPSK switch logic]]

- **S1 / S2** controlled by data bit (100 Hz baseband in example)
- Carrier at 1 kHz: **in-phase** and **180° out-of-phase** paths

- Data **0** → S2 on → inverted carrier at output

## Slide 19 — Raised-cosine filter & ISI

![[99-ATTACHMENTS/EGE321/lesson7c/lesson7c-slide-19.png|Slide 19 — Raised cosine pulse shaping]]

- **Raised-cosine filter** used for pulse-shaping in digital modulation

**Given:** 2 Mbps data on 900 MHz carrier, BPSK, no baseband filter.

2. Calculate RF mainlobe bandwidth
3. Allowed bandwidth 2.6 MHz — how to use raised cosine?
4. Effect of roll-off factor α on bandwidth and ISI

---

## Slide 21 — Raised-cosine example (solution i–iii)

![[99-ATTACHMENTS/EGE321/lesson7c/lesson7c-slide-21.png|Slide 21 — Spectrum sketch and bandwidth]]

- **(ii)** RF mainlobe bandwidth = **2 × symbol rate = 4 MHz**
- **(iii)** To fit **2.6 MHz**: use raised cosine with **α = 0.3**
  - RF bandwidth = **(1 + α) × 2 MHz = 2.6 MHz**

---

## Slide 22 — Roll-off factor trade-off

![[99-ATTACHMENTS/EGE321/lesson7c/lesson7c-slide-22.png|Slide 22 — Alpha vs bandwidth and ISI]]

- **fc ± Rs(1+α)/2** defines band edges (example: fc = 900 MHz)
- **α increases** → bandwidth ↑, ISI ↓
- **α decreases** → bandwidth ↓, ISI ↑

## Flashcards

- **Q:** QPSK bandwidth efficiency vs BPSK? **A:** 2× — 2 bits per symbol.
- **Q:** Conventional QPSK phase transition issue? **A:** Can pass through zero (180° jumps) → envelope variation.
- **Q:** π/4 QPSK advantage? **A:** No transitions through zero → lower envelope variation.
- **Q:** 16-QAM bits per symbol? **A:** 4 bits; symbol rate = Rb/4.
- **Q:** Raised-cosine purpose? **A:** Pulse shaping to reduce ISI.
- **Q:** RF bandwidth with raised cosine? **A:** (1 + α) × symbol rate (one-sided formulation varies — use course formula).
- **Q:** Eye diagram shows? **A:** Sampling point, SNR, jitter, distortion.
- **Q:** BER full form? **A:** Bit Error Rate.
